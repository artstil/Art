## 2025-03-09 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found multiple icon-only navigation and modal control buttons lacking screen reader descriptions and visible keyboard focus outlines within the main application. These included calendar controls, mobile playback controls, a floating action button, and modal close triggers. Without explicit `aria-label`s, these interactable elements are completely opaque to assistive technologies, and lack of visual focus styles makes them difficult to target for keyboard users.
**Action:** When implementing or updating icon-only interactive elements, always ensure they are paired with a descriptive `aria-label` attribute and clear `focus-visible` ring utilities (`focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2`) to maintain accessible interaction paths.
