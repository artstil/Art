## 2026-08-23 - Critical ARIA Labels Missing on Icon-Only Modals and Controls\n**Learning:** Identified a recurrent accessibility issue across this app's components, where multiple floating action buttons, inline play/pause states, modal close buttons (X), and calendar chevron navigators lacked ARIA labels and title tooltips. These hidden interactive points make navigation difficult for screen readers.\n**Action:** Proactively scan for and apply `aria-label` and `title` to all icon-only control buttons, especially within common floating states and modal headers across the application.
